## User-generated content (UGC policy)

The only content one student can show another is the **nickname**. What protects students:

- A word filter (`NicknameFilter`) blocks bad names when they are chosen.
- Staff can remove a nickname in Nova; that also takes the student off the leaderboard.
- Nicknames are optional; students without one never appear to others.
- In-app reporting of a nickname: see QUESTIONS.md #45 (owner decision; recommended before launch).
